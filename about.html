About

A little about the artist behind the characters.

Hi, I'm Nyx

I'm a digital artist from Croatia, and I've been drawing since I was 7, so that's around 18 years of practice. Characters and people are my favourite thing to draw.

My favourite style is animated, but I can work in almost any style, so if you have something specific in mind, just ask.

What I draw

  • Furry commissions: headshots, half-body, full-body, chibi
  • Original characters
  • Game fan art and animated characters

Process and tools

Everything is drawn digitally by hand in Procreate on my iPad.
